## 📌 Problem Overview

Every harvest season, millions of tonnes of agricultural crop residues (paddy straw, sugarcane bagasse, cotton stalks, pruning waste) are burned or discarded, creating toxic air pollution, soil degradation, and lost economic value. Simultaneously, nearby farmers, composters, nursery operators, and mushroom cultivators spend thousands procuring expensive raw organic inputs from commercial suppliers.

**AgriVicta BioLoop AI** bridges this gap by providing an end-to-end circular workflow: from identifying residue suitability with transparent agricultural rules, to matching materials on **MittiBank**, calculating true cost savings, and verifying physical lifecycle transfers.
